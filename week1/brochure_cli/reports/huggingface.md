## About Hugging Face

**The AI community building the future.**

Hugging Face is a pioneering platform and community in artificial intelligence and machine learning, fostering collaboration on models, datasets, and AI-driven applications. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Paris, France, Hugging Face is dedicated to creating an open, ethical AI future by empowering machine learning engineers, scientists, and users worldwide.

The Hugging Face Hub serves as a central place where the global machine learning community can share, explore, discover, and experiment with open-source ML models, datasets, and tools. With over 2 million models and more than 500,000 datasets, Hugging Face offers an extensive repository that spans multiple AI modalities including text, images, video, audio, and even 3D.

## Products & Services

### Community and Collaboration Platform
- Host and collaborate on unlimited public models, datasets, and AI applications.
- Share your projects to build your machine learning portfolio and profile.
- Access cutting-edge open-source libraries such as:
  - Transformers (156k stars): State-of-the-art AI models for NLP and more.
  - Diffusers (33k stars): Leading diffusion models for image, video, and audio generation.
  - Tokenizers, PEFT (parameter-efficient fine-tuning), Accelerate, and many other tools designed for training, fine-tuning, and deploying AI models.
