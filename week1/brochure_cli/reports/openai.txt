About OpenAI

OpenAI is a pioneering AI research and deployment company with a mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI)—highly autonomous systems capable of outperforming humans at most economically valuable work—benefits all of humanity. Founded in 2015, OpenAI combines cutting-edge research with real-world deployment to accelerate the path toward safe and beneficial AI for everyone.

OpenAI operates as a hybrid organization, consisting of the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation and the for-profit OpenAI Group, governed to prioritize broad societal benefits and transparency. Their vision advocates global cooperation, long-term safety, and leadership in AI technology to ensure AGI's responsible development.

Products & Technologies

OpenAI builds advanced AI models and tools across multiple domains:
- GPT Series: Industry-leading language models like GPT-5.2 and GPT-5.3-Codex for versatile, high-performance text generation, understanding, and reasoning.
- Codex: An AI coding partner that assists in building, debugging, and shipping software, with specialized multi-agent workflows.
- Sora: A natively multimodal model for generating hyperrealistic videos with motion, sound, and characters from simple prompts.
- OpenAI o-series: Reasoning models specialized in complex scientific, mathematical, and logical problem-solving.
- Image & Audio AI: Models like DALL·E for image generation and Whisper for advanced speech recognition, enabling creative visual and voice applications.
- API Platform: The fastest, most powerful API ecosystem supporting text, image, audio, and vision inputs for real-time AI applications.

Commitment to Safety

Safety and responsibility are central to OpenAI’s work:
- Building AI systems that teach right from wrong and filter harmful content.
- Rigorous internal testing complemented by expert external evaluations.
- Continuous improvement based on real-world feedback.
- Focus on protecting vulnerable populations (children, privacy), preventing bias, combating misinformation, and safeguarding critical processes like elections.

OpenAI openly shares system cards and research transparency for their models, detailing mitigation strategies for risks like misleading outputs, unauthorized likeness generation, and cybersecurity threats.
