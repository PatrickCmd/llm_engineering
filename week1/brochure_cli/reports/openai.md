## About OpenAI

OpenAI is a leading artificial intelligence research and deployment company with the mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI)—highly autonomous systems that surpass human capabilities in economically valuable work—benefits all of humanity. Founded in 2015, OpenAI operates both as a nonprofit foundation and a for-profit group, governed by the OpenAI Foundation to balance innovation with ethical responsibility.

Their research focuses on creating safe, powerful AI tools such as the GPT series, Codex, and visionary multimodal systems, with a long-term commitment to safety, transparency, and cooperation in AI development globally.

---

## Company Vision and Charter

- **Mission**: Build safe and beneficial AGI or support others in achieving this goal.
- **Broadly Distributed Benefits**: Avoid harmful uses and concentrated power by ensuring benefits for all.
- **Long-term Safety**: Invest deeply in AI safety research and foster community-wide adoption of safe practices.
- **Technical Leadership**: Advance cutting-edge AI technology to influence societal impacts positively.
- **Cooperative Orientation**: Collaborate extensively with policymakers, researchers, and industry leaders globally.

## Products & Solutions

OpenAI offers a rich ecosystem of AI products and services powered by their frontier AI models:

### Key Products
- **GPT-5 Series**: State-of-the-art models offering powerful natural language understanding, generation, and reasoning across text, image, and audio.
- **Codex**: An AI coding partner designed to drive real engineering workflows with multitasking agents, enabling teams to build, debug, and ship software faster.

- **OpenAI Frontier**: An enterprise AI platform for deploying AI agents integrated into business systems with comprehensive governance, security, and auditing.

### Industry Solutions
- Financial services, healthcare, life sciences, retails, manufacturing, energy, and communications sectors harness OpenAI’s AI to optimize operations, enhance customer experiences, support research, and drive innovation.

## Safety and Responsibility

OpenAI places safety at the core of AI development:
- **Rigorous Testing and Alignment**: AI systems are designed and tuned with extensive evaluations, human feedback, and red teaming to reduce risks like bias, misinformation, and misuse.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Safety is an ongoing process with real-world feedback loops.
- **Focused Area Protection**: Special attention to child safety, privacy, misinformation like deep fakes, and electoral integrity.
- **Transparency**: Open publication of safety frameworks, system cards, and proactive sharing of research and policies.
