# Sunbird AI Brochure

---

## Who We Are

**Sunbird AI** is a pioneering artificial intelligence research organization dedicated to developing AI technologies tailored to solve African problems. Based in Kampala, Uganda, we apply cutting-edge AI research to societal challenges, empowering local communities through innovative, impactful solutions.

---

## Our Mission

To create AI-powered tools and systems that address critical African development challenges in inclusive and scalable ways, focusing on language accessibility, environmental monitoring, public service delivery, and sustainable development.

---

## Flagship Product

### Sunflower Multilingual Assistant

- **A multilingual AI assistant that supports 31 Ugandan languages.**
- Delivers accurate **translations, summaries, and explanations** in local languages.
- Offers **conversational capabilities** tailored to Ugandan linguistic diversity.
- Supported by open research: detailed in the paper "Sunflower: A New Approach to Expanding Coverage of African Languages in Large Language Models."
- Accessible via the **Sunflower API**.

---

## Key Projects and Impact

### African Language Technology

- Developed the **Sunbird Translate system** supporting automatic translation between English and five major Ugandan languages, including Acholi, Ateso, Luganda, Lugbara, and Runyankole.
- Created the **SALT dataset**: 25,000 sentences translated across six Ugandan languages, focusing on healthcare, agriculture, and society.
- Developed **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** models using crowdsourced data—an innovative, scalable approach enabling speech technology in predominantly spoken local languages.
- Projects aim to empower those excluded by language barriers to access information, services, and participate in decision making.

### Citizen Feedback Systems

- Enable citizens to provide **voice feedback in various local languages**, improving public service responsiveness.
- Partnership with organizations such as **SEMA Uganda** and **TRAC FM** to make feedback more accessible and analyzable.
- Support Uganda’s **Parish Development Model (PDM)** by enabling two-way communication between government and rural communities despite language barriers.

### Green Electrification Planning

- Collaborate with the **German Agency for International Cooperation (GIZ)** and Uganda’s Ministry of Energy on AI-driven tools to plan renewable energy site locations.
- Support the national goal of connecting 10 million households to electricity by 2030.
- Utilize satellite imagery and machine learning to forecast community development and optimal electrification strategies.

### Environmental Sensing and Noise Pollution Monitoring

- Working with **Entebbe City Authority** and **Kampala Capital City Authority (KCCA)** to collect real-time urban noise data.
- Develop hardware and software solutions to monitor, analyze, and mitigate noise pollution—a growing public health concern.
- Promote urban planning and public awareness through open data and community engagement.
