## About Sunbird AI

Sunbird AI is a pioneering artificial intelligence research organization dedicated to applying AI solutions to challenges uniquely faced by African communities. Based in Kampala, Uganda, Sunbird AI focuses on leveraging cutting-edge AI research to create scalable and impactful technologies that address social, economic, and environmental issues across the continent. Their work is deeply rooted in collaboration with academic institutions and local partners to foster innovation that benefits society.

---

## Company Culture

Sunbird AI fosters a culture of innovation, impact, and collaboration. The team is made up of passionate experts in machine learning, software engineering, data science, environmental sensing, and language technology, many of whom are affiliated with Makerere University and other prestigious institutions globally. The organization embraces a mission-driven approach focused on societal benefit, social good, and inclusion.

They emphasize continuous learning and real-world AI application, supporting ongoing research, development, and deployment of solutions addressing African problems. The company supports a Fellows Program that invites passionate individuals and researchers to contribute and collaborate on impactful projects.

## Flagship Projects & Innovations

### Sunflower Multilingual Assistant  
- AI-powered assistant supporting 31 Ugandan languages  
- Provides translation, explanations, summaries, and conversational abilities in local languages  
- Impactfully bridges language barriers for millions of Ugandans  

### African Language Technology  
- Development of the SALT dataset: 25,000 sentences translated into six Ugandan languages  
- Sunbird Translate: state-of-the-art multilingual translation system for local languages  
- Speech technology innovations including crowdsourced Text-to-Speech (TTS) for Luganda — the first of its kind available openly  

### Environmental Sensing & Noise Pollution  
- Collaborations with city authorities of Kampala and Entebbe to analyze noise pollution’s impact on public health  
- Deployment of custom sensors to collect real-time acoustic data for urban planning and mitigation purposes  

### Green Electrification Planning  
- AI-driven renewable energy site identification to support Uganda’s National Electrification Strategy (NES)  
- Partnership with the German Agency for International Cooperation (GIZ) and Ministry of Energy to increase rural electrification access  

### Citizen Feedback Systems  
- AI-powered voice feedback tools deployed in hospitals, police stations, and radio platforms  
- Facilitates citizen engagement in multiple local languages for better public service delivery and governance transparency  
- Collaboration with Ministry of ICT and National Guidance on inclusive communication for rural development programs

## Careers and Fellowship Opportunities

Sunbird AI offers a competitive six-month Fellowship Program designed for researchers, developers, and AI practitioners passionate about social impact. Fellows work closely with Sunbird teams on projects aligned with their expertise and societal goals. The program supports flexible participation (part-time/full-time, remote/on-site) and fosters mentorship by Sunbird’s expert advisors.

**How to apply:**  
- Submit a motivation letter detailing your alignment with Sunbird’s mission  
- Present a project plan focused on AI for social good  
- Optional supporting materials: GitHub links, research papers, recommendation letters  

Join Sunbird AI to grow your career applying AI to meaningful problems affecting Africa's communities.
